# Afterburn editions

Generated by `tools/make-editions.sh`. Do not hand-edit; change the script or an edition's `tools/edition-extras/<name>/edition.conf` and rerun it.

Each edition keeps the base palette structure and five hue-rotated alternative stills, with a bespoke default still and signature live loop. Orange's three live loops remain exclusive to the base theme. See docs/wallpapers/README.md for artwork and motion details.
Each also carries its own cool and hot heat wallpapers in `heat-wallpapers/`, which the `afterburn` command swaps in at those heat levels.

Lite is the light edition: ink on warm paper instead of light on black. Its palette is hand-made (`tools/edition-extras/lite/colors.toml`, every text colour at least 4.5:1 on the paper), the six base stills are printed onto the paper colour, and it has no glow: a short warm shadow instead. A light wallpaper would jar in the dark themes, so Lite's art is not copied into the shared `backgrounds/` folder.

## How they move

Each edition ships an `edition.conf`. The theme-set hook and the `afterburn` command read it for the active theme, check every value, and use it as the default for these settings. A value you save yourself in `~/.config/afterburn/afterburn.conf` (or with `afterburn set`) always wins; `afterburn unset KEY` hands the setting back to the edition.

| Edition | Cursor effect | Border turn | Glow range | Dim | Lock embers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| afterburn (base) | embers | slow, 10 s | 14 | 0.08 | rise |
| voltage | lightning | fast, 2.5 s | 12 | 0.06 | spark |
| cyber | cyber | slow, 10 s | 17 | 0.12 | fall |
| overdrive | neon | medium, 5 s | 22 | 0.04 | pulse |
| redline | katana | still | 8 | 0.15 | burst |
| phantom | hologram | still | 24 | 0.18 | drift |
| lite (light) | embers | still | 10 | 0.03 | drift |

Lock embers: `rise` drifts up (the base), `fall` rains down, `drift` floats and fades, `spark` crackles, `pulse` swells on a beat, `burst` fires sudden bursts between stillness.
